Field Journal. Shelly keeps a naturalist's sketchbook: cream paper, ink line work, watercolour that misses the lines on purpose. Signature motion: everything inks itself in, line first, wash blooming up a beat later. The screens demo themselves: the button presses with an ink ripple and bursts a little firework of strawberries that fades before the text, Shelly answers with the peace sign, the slider turtles wander until you grab one, the dashboard leafs through week, month and year with the numbers counting between them, and every so often the hare tears past. CSS plus a little vanilla JS.
