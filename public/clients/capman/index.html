    01 · Method

    What I checked, and what I could not see.

    What this is

    An independent review of the public capman.es, carried out on 5 August 2026. Every screenshot in this document was taken from the live site on that date. Every figure from outside Capman carries its source.

    Pages walked: homepage, TOEIC hub, a bookable product page, the centre finder, the company page, the blog and the offers page.

    What I could not see

    No analytics, no Search Console, no CRM, no sales data. So this document says nothing about your traffic, conversion rate or revenue, and does not pretend to. The first ask in this deck is read-only access to those numbers.

    Load speed and Core Web Vitals are not measured here. Google's testing API was rate limited on the day and a guessed number is worse than an admitted gap.

    Honest note

    Prototype screenshots pending

    Three working HTML prototypes exist from July. Their files are not in the folder I can reach today, so the design visuals here come from the Figma record of that work, not from the running prototypes. Flagged rather than faked.

    02 · The company

    Capman sells certainty. The website sells discounts.

    Exclusive ETS distributor for Spain and Portugal. TOEIC, TOEFL, TFI, PRAXIS, VISA UK, and your own 360ºLPT. Two very different buyers, one website.

    Buyer one

    The candidate

    A student, a professional, someone facing an oposición. Needs to know which exam, what level, when, where and how much. Buys today if the answer is clear.

    Buyer two

    The organisation

    HR, universities, colegios, academias. Buys on proof and process. Needs a proposal, not a checkout.

    The asset

    Exclusivity

    Being the exclusive ETS distributor for two countries is the strongest sentence in the business. It appears nowhere in the first screen of the homepage.

    03 · Credit where it is due

    Five things the site already does better than its competitors.

    • Real bookable sessions. Live availability by country, province and date, down to plazas libres. Most competitors hide this behind a login.
    • Published prices. TOEIC 97,30 € to 141,75 €. TOEFL 119 € to 349 €. 360ºLPT 104,30 €. Pearson PTE, British Council IELTS and LanguageCert publish no price publicly.
    • Serious B2B proof. Named case studies including Canon, Air France and Taisei, testimonials from Exolum and CEMEX, the IPSOS study for ETS.
    • A living blog. Twelve posts back to August 2025, newest 8 July 2026.
    • Honest speed promises. Results in 7 días naturales, 15 for Speaking and Writing.
    04 · The problem

    The site has every answer a buyer needs. It just makes them dig.

    Dates, prices, availability, proof, recognition. All of it exists. Almost none of it is visible before the third click.

    This is the good kind of problem. Capman does not need new inventory, new products or a new value proposition. It needs the ones it already has to be findable. That is a design and structure job, and it is measurable.

    05 · The pattern, in one picture

    Five questions every buyer asks, and how deep each answer is buried.

    Every one of these answers already exists on the site. The findings that follow are this chart, one row at a time, with the evidence beside each.

    05 · Finding 01

    Three of the five main navigation items go nowhere.

    ExámenesFormaciónServicios y pruebas paraCentrosBlog
    Evidence

    All three carry href="https://capman.es/". Clicking a category returns the visitor to the homepage they were trying to leave. There is no exam hub to land on, and no page to rank for the category term.

    The fix is three real hub pages, each with the category's products, a plain comparison and a single next step. Content and template work on the existing Drupal, not a rebuild.

    06 · Finding 02

    Six exam families, and no way to work out which one you need.

    TOEIC, TOEFL, TFI, 360ºLPT, VISA UK, PRAXIS. There is no comparison table, no chooser and no "which exam do I need" route anywhere on the site.

    Cambridge publishes a free level check and a full CEFR chart. Pearson PTE runs a "Which PTE test is right for me?" page routing four products by purpose. LanguageCert has an explorer and a 20-question quiz.

    Why it matters

    The visitor doing the hardest thinking is the one closest to buying. Capman leaves that thinking entirely to them, then asks them to pick from six unfamiliar acronyms.

    07 · Where this goes

    A three-question chooser, and the first question answered on the homepage.

    What it does

    Three questions: what do you need it for, what level do you think you are, in person or at home. Out comes one recommendation, one price and the next available date.

    In the reimagined structure the first question is answered inline on the homepage as six plain links, so a visitor who already knows never enters a wizard at all, and one who does not gets routed in a single click.

    Degrades without JavaScript

    The chooser is links and stacked questions, not an app. It works with scripts blocked, which matters for a site that must stay indexable.

    08 · Finding 03

    The dates exist. They are three clicks deep, and nothing above them says so.

    Real sessions with places left sit on the deepest product page, behind País, Provincia and Fecha de examen filters. Not on the homepage. Not in the navigation. Not on the exam pages.

    "When can I sit this, and where" is the most common question a candidate has. The site answers it well, in the last place they look.

    The competitive point

    British Council publishes Aptis dates, venues and prices openly across 40 or more Spanish locations. Capman has comparable inventory and hides it. Surfacing it is a presentation decision, the data is already in Drupal Commerce.

    09 · Where this goes

    Put the answer on the page, as a register, not a card grid.

    01

    Every exam compared on the same five facts, so the visitor stops guessing which one they need.

    02

    Availability and the next date belong here, pulled from the same Commerce data already powering the booking table.

    03

    This single component answers the two questions the current site answers last: which exam, and when.

    10 · Finding 04

    The level is the product. It is mentioned once, in the small print.

    The MCERL levels appear on the product page as a line of prose: su equiparación con el MCERL en los niveles A1, A2, B1, B2 y C1. There is no score-to-band table anywhere on the site, and the 360ºLPT page promises an "official level certificate" without naming a level at all.

    Almost nobody wants a TOEIC score. They want a B2 for an oposición, a C1 for a master's, a documented level for a promotion. Cambridge publishes its whole mapping as a downloadable chart, which is one reason people default to it when unsure.

    Needs Capman input
    The exact score bands must come from ETS documentation, not from me. Capman already holds this content internally.

    12 · Finding 06

    The homepage opens on a carousel of competing offers.

    On the homepage today

    Four slides, and these calls to action before any product: Ver descuentos, Nota Online, Accede a la oferta aquí, Becas TOEIC 4 Skills, Becas TOEIC L&R.

    Effect one. A carousel asks the visitor to wait. Most never see slides two to four, so four messages compete for one slot and none wins.

    Effect two. Five promotional calls to action before a single exam means the first decision offered is which discount to chase, not which exam to sit.

    Effect three. A permanently discount-led homepage teaches the market to wait for the next offer. That is a margin question, and only Capman's sales data can size it.

    13 · Where this goes

    One statement, and the choice made in the same breath.

    The move

    Borrowed openly from Pearson PTE, whose destination chooser sits in the hero with an escape hatch for people who are not sure yet.

    The ground

    Set on the engraving-green security paper Capman actually prints certificates on. The product becomes the art direction.

    The discipline

    No equal-card grids, no pill chips, no stock photography, no carousel. Those bans are what stop a redesign looking like every other template.

    14 · Finding 07

    The blog is current. The homepage makes it look abandoned.

    On the blog itself, the same day

    8 July 2026 · 9 June 2026 · 1 June 2026 · 11 May 2026 · 20 April 2026 · 2 March 2026, twelve posts in all.

    Real work is being done and the homepage throws it away. A visitor checking whether this company is still active sees content up to three years old on the page they land on.

    The recent posts carry the strongest proof the company has: the Región de Murcia officially recognising 360ºLPT in April 2026, UAM and UPM programmes, UNIMIAMI adopting the test. Institutional validation, sitting where nobody looks.

    This one is close to free to fix.

    15 · Finding 08

    The trust block is in English on a Spanish-only site.

    The section title is English, the sentence under it is Spanish, and the four statistics are labelled in English. The figures animate up on scroll, so the numbers get the attention while the words get none.

    These are ETS's numbers, not Capman's, and nothing on the block says so. A visitor could reasonably read 70 years and 9000 locations as Capman's own.

    Two separate problems

    One is language consistency, which is a translation task. The other is attribution, which is a trust question. The site's strongest borrowed proof is also its least clearly sourced.

    Needs Capman input
    Whether these figures may be restated, and in what form, is an ETS licensing question I cannot answer from outside.

    16 · Finding 09

    The best page on the site sits under a label nobody would click.

    capman.es/empresas carries four case studies including Canon, Air France and Taisei, testimonials from HR at Exolum and CEMEX, the IPSOS "Why English Matters" study for ETS, and a no-obligation form: Contáctanos sin compromiso.

    That is a credible enterprise sales page hiding inside a consumer shop, reached through a dropdown called "Servicios y pruebas para".

    The structural problem

    B2C and B2B share one navigation, one tone and one cart. An HR director buying 200 tests does not add to basket, and a student does not want a proposal.

    17 · Where this goes

    Two doors. One for a person, one for an organisation.

    Consequence

    The organisations track converts to a proposal request, never to a cart, and leads with the case studies that already exist.

    Consequence

    The candidate track keeps the shop, the prices and the booking table, without enterprise language getting in the way.

    Cost

    One line in the top bar and a landing page. No new inventory, no new products, no migration.

    18 · Finding 10

    Every trust asset is real, and every one of them is buried.

    • Certificate verification and security paper. A page proving certificates cannot be faked, linked only from the footer. In a market with real fraud anxiety, that is a headline, not a footnote.
    • CRUE and ACLES recognition. Stated on product pages. Not on the homepage, not in the navigation, not in search-visible headings.
    • Oposiciones acceptance. Correos and Renfe named on one product page. That is the exact phrase a large audience searches for.
    • Regional recognition. Región de Murcia officially recognised 360ºLPT in April 2026, announced in a blog post and nowhere else.
    • ALTE and EALTA membership. Two logos in the footer, at the very bottom, in grey.

    capman.es/verificacion-de-resultados-y-papel-de-seguridad · capman.es/360olpt · capman.es/examenes/toeic-4skills · capman.es/blog. All checked 5 Aug 2026.

    19 · Where this goes

    Show the certificate. It is the product, and nobody has seen it.

    Capman prints on security paper with anti-fraud measures and runs a public verification service. That is a genuine differentiator against at-home online tests, and it is currently a footer link.

    Putting the document itself on the page, annotated, does three jobs at once: it explains what the buyer receives, it proves the certificate is hard to fake, and it gives the brand something to look like that no competitor can copy.

    Marked as pending

    Final artwork, the security-paper "10 medidas" and logo-usage permissions are all data-verify items awaiting Capman input. Nothing here claims a measure that has not been confirmed.

    20 · Under the bonnet

    Three structural issues that quietly cost search traffic.

    01 · URLs

    Four different patterns

    /examenes-toeic, /examenes/tfi, /360olpt, /praxis-tests. No rule a person or a search engine can predict.

    02 · Duplication

    Two live URLs for TOEFL

    The navigation points at /examenes-toefl while the body and footer point at /examenes-oficiales-de-ingles-toefl. Both resolve. Authority is split between them.

    03 · Empty states

    Search that starts empty

    The centre finder shows nothing until the visitor picks a filter. An empty result is the most expensive screen on any site.

    Not yet measured

    Load speed, Core Web Vitals, accessibility conformance and real mobile behaviour were not measured for this document. They are week-one tasks with access, not claims I will make without data.

    21 · The evidence

    What the published research says about the gap between "on the site" and "found".

    Why buyers abandon a checkout · Baymard, 2025

    Average documented cart abandonment is 70.22% across 50 studies. The top reason is not price. It is information arriving late.

    Conversion against load time · Portent, 2022

    5.6 million sessions. An honest caveat: Deloitte's 2020 lead-generation sample saw engagement rise but conversion fall 1.9% with faster loads, so I will not promise a speed-driven lift before measuring Capman's own baseline.

    The reliable finding across all of it is not "make it faster". It is that buyers abandon when answers arrive late: costs, options, availability, next steps. That is precisely the Capman pattern, which is why the fixes here are ordered by how early they move an answer, not by how much code they need.

    baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate · baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields · baymard.com/blog/current-state-product-list-and-filtering · portent.com/blog/analytics/research-site-speed-hurting-everyones-revenue.htm · thinkwithgoogle.com, Milliseconds Make Millions, Deloitte 2020.

    22 · The first ask

    Before any redesign: read-only access to what already measures this site.

    Everything in this review was diagnosed from the outside. The inside view exists already, it just needs sharing. Ten minutes of granting access turns every benchmark in this deck into Capman's own baseline.

    If no behaviour tool is installed, Microsoft Clarity is free and takes an afternoon to add. Session recordings will show exactly where candidates stall in the booking table, rather than me inferring it.

    Why this comes first

    The scenario model on the next slide runs on placeholders today. With these four accesses it runs on real traffic, real conversion and real order values, and its predictions become defensible in front of a board.

    • Google Analytics 4, read-only. Traffic, sources, conversion rate, where the funnel actually leaks, seasonality around convocatorias.
    • Search Console. What Google already ranks, which queries bring candidates, and what the nav-link and duplicate-URL findings actually cost.
    • Microsoft Clarity or Hotjar, if installed. Recordings and rage-click maps of the carousel, the filters and the checkout.
    • A Drupal Commerce order export, last 24 months. Real average order value, discount usage against the 50% campaigns, B2C against B2B mix.

    23 · The plan

    Four phases. Revenue work first, redesign second.

    Phase 0 · 2 weeks

    Measure

    Analytics, Search Console, funnel baselines. Audit the current Drupal build and its integrations. Agree the numbers we will be judged on before touching anything.

    Phase 1 · 4 to 6 weeks

    Fix, on the current site

    Working navigation, real category hubs, availability surfaced, an exam chooser, a comparison register, an MCERL page, homepage blog fixed. No rebuild, measurable within the quarter.

    Phase 2 · in parallel

    Design the new site

    Information architecture, design system, page patterns, scan-first exam pages, a separate organisations track. Three directions already exist, see the next slides.

    Phase 3 · after discovery

    Build

    Retheme Drupal or move the front end. That decision belongs to Capman and should be made with discovery findings in hand, not before.

    The principle

    Nothing in phase 1 depends on the redesign, and nothing in the redesign is wasted by phase 1. If the rebuild is postponed for a year, the phase 1 work still earns its money.

    24 · Direction one

    Same structure, done properly. The low-risk option.

    Forty-two pages, keeping the current information architecture, with real Spanish copy taken from the live site and nothing invented. No carousel, one message per section, every internal link resolving.

    Drawn in true greyscale on purpose, so the conversation is about structure rather than colour. Figma "Capman", page Design, frames 8:4, 8:7 and 8:16. The working HTML exists but its files are not reachable from this session, flagged on the method slide.

    25 · Direction two

    "Papel de seguridad". The same structure, with a face nobody else has.

    One field colour, the engraving green of the security paper, carrying real guilloche linework. Ecru paper for the light sections. One warm ember accent, deliberately a different temperature, driving every action.

    Editorial serif at display scale, so the page is carried by type and by the product itself rather than by photography Capman does not own.

    Where the ideas came from

    The live proof bar from Ramp. The two-door audience split from Cambridge. The chooser inside the hero from Pearson PTE. Each borrowed on purpose, from sites that already prove the pattern works.

    There is a third direction, variant B, a full 41-page reimagination with a different type system. Its Figma record does not exist yet, so it is named here rather than shown.

    26 · Straight talk

    What I do not yet know, and the rules I work by.

    The one real unknown

    The ETS integration. How results, registrations and certificates move between Capman's Drupal and ETS cannot be assessed from outside. It needs the codebase and ETS documentation.

    I will not quote it blind. It is a discovery task with a fixed time box, and the findings decide the rest of the plan. Anyone who prices that integration without looking is guessing.

    Payments are more predictable. Redsys and Stripe both have sandboxes, so checkout can be tested properly before anything reaches customers. IVA handling, Verifactu invoicing, webhooks and refunds all need explicit attention.

    Non-negotiables

    • A staging copy and version control for any work near the live shop. The site takes money every day, so it never gets edited live.
    • An independent review of payments and security before launch. Not by me, by someone whose job is to disagree with me.
    • No production customer data on any development machine. GDPR is not a formality.
    • Every change measured against the phase 0 baseline. If something does not work, we say so and change it.
    27 · The gap

    There is no designer and no development team. That is the actual problem to solve.

    Everything in this document is worth nothing without someone who owns it, week after week, and is accountable for the numbers.

    What I would own

    Information architecture, interface design, the design system, front-end implementation, and the measurement that says whether it worked.

    What I would coordinate

    Drupal back-end work, the ETS integration and the pre-launch security review, with specialists brought in for those parts and briefed properly.

    What Capman keeps

    Every file, every repository, every decision documented. No lock-in, no black box. If I stop tomorrow, the next person picks it up from written specs.

    28 · The arrangement

    Freelance, on a monthly retainer, with the door open to something more permanent.

    The shape

    A fixed monthly retainer

    A set number of days per month, scoped at the start of each cycle against the phase plan, reported at the end of it. Predictable for Capman, sustainable for me.

    Larger phases

    Quoted separately

    Discovery and the build are scoped and quoted as projects, so a big piece of work never quietly eats the retainer that keeps the site improving.

    Longer term

    Open to a permanent role

    If the work proves itself and Capman wants the function in-house, I am open to that conversation. The retainer is the sensible way for both sides to find out.

    Commercial terms

    To complete before sending
    Day rate, retainer band, notice period and the phase 0 fixed price go here. Deliberately left out of the review itself, so the findings are read on their merits first.

    29 · If we start

    What the first thirty days look like.

    • Access and baseline. Analytics, Search Console, the Drupal codebase, the staging environment. Measure speed, Core Web Vitals and accessibility properly. Agree the four numbers we will report on.
    • Funnel truth. Walk the full purchase path as a real candidate and as a real HR buyer. Document every step where the answer arrives late. Interview whoever answers the phone, because they already know.
    • Ship the free wins. Navigation that navigates, the homepage blog module pointed at current posts, the TOEFL URL duplication resolved, trust assets surfaced. Small, visible, immediate.
    • Availability and the chooser, designed. The two changes most likely to move revenue, specified and ready to build, with the measurement plan attached.

    By day thirty Capman has a measured baseline, a shorter path to purchase and evidence about whether this works. That is a cheap way to find out.

    30 · In one line

    Capman already has the inventory, the prices, the proof and the recognition. The website is the only part not pulling its weight.

    Fixing that does not need a rebuild to start. It needs someone to own it.

    Next step

    Two weeks of phase 0, fixed price. At the end of it Capman has a measured baseline and a scoped plan, and can stop there with something useful either way.
